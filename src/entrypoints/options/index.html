How to use

  1. Write your reminder and press Save. It is stored only on this device.
  2. Open or navigate within YouTube. The gate appears before you can interact with the page.
  3. Choose Yes, continue to allow just this page, or No — I'd rather affirm to open the affirmation timer and close this tab.
  4. Every new video or YouTube page asks again. There is no permanent "allow forever" option.

Your reminder is stored locally in this browser profile. Nothing is sent anywhere.
